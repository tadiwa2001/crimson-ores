Who We Are

Engineering a Smarter Future for Mining

Crimson Ores is a Zimbabwean mining-technology startup developing practical, technology-driven solutions for the challenges that shape every mine — from planning and blasting to ventilation, ground control and ore handling.

We combine mining engineering with automation and data to make operations smarter, safer, more efficient and more sustainable.
